Why the system performs on site

Designed to support predictable coordination from fabrication through final fastening

Protected steel for long-term corrosion resistance

G90 hot-dip galvanized steel to ASTM A653/A653M by default; alternative coatings such as 55% aluminum-zinc to ASTM A792/A792M or zinc-aluminum-magnesium-coated steel available on request, subject to engineering and availability

Controlled fastening logic with rivets

Controlled rivet-based assembly detail for ECOLIT framing. Rivet-based assembly reduces field fastening errors and keeps crews inside a clearer install logic

Prefab profiles with utility-ready openings

Prefab steel profile with ready-made utility opening. Openings are prepared before the site tries to solve MEP conflicts with improvised cutting

Clean assembly with minimal field improvisation

Standard assembly is designed to minimize or eliminate field cutting, which helps protect schedule and consistency

Lightweight components for easier handling

Lightweight panels reduce handling strain and lift risk while keeping the site workflow more manageable

Factory geometry for cleaner finishes

Factory-formed geometry improves alignment, finish quality, and material predictability for the builder

FAQ

Builder questions answered with clear, project-ready guidance

Do you provide stamped structural packages?

Yes. ECOLIT can provide a stamped structural set with calculations for the supplied system and design criteria, based on project scope and jurisdiction requirements.

Can my crew install the system?

Yes. Many builders use their own crews with ECOLIT guidance, onboarding, and supervisor support when the project benefits from added field coordination.

Do you support California permits and Title 24?

Yes. California projects can be supported with structural coordination, Title 24 inputs, and documentation that keeps the permit path organized.

What does the inspection package include?

The package can include field checklists, hold points, visibility requirements, and concise reference materials for inspectors and site teams.

How do review comments get handled?

ECOLIT can respond quickly with clarifications, updated references, and comment support that helps keep the project moving.

Are white-label materials available for builders?

Yes. Builders can use white-label proposal materials, homeowner one-pagers, and comparison content for owner conversations.

Can pricing be held during preconstruction?

Approved projects can be supported with a 90-day price stability window when applicable to scope and scheduling.

Do you provide turnkey erection as well?

Yes. Builders can choose the support model that fits the project, including supervisor-backed installation or turnkey erection.

Are utility openings coordinated in the system?

Utility pathways can be planned within the framing logic so site coordination stays cleaner from design through rough-in.

How is delivery coordinated?

Delivery planning can be aligned with project sequencing, site access, and installation needs to support a predictable handoff.
