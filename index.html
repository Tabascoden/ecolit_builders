Why The System Works On Site

ECOLIT is engineered to reduce improvisation, fastening errors, and finishing drift.

Protected steel for long-term corrosion resistance

G90 hot-dip galvanized steel to ASTM A653/A653M by default; alternative coatings such as 55% aluminum-zinc to ASTM A792/A792M or zinc-aluminum-magnesium-coated steel available on request, subject to engineering and availability.

Controlled fastening logic with rivets

Rivet-based assembly reduces field fastening errors and keeps crews inside a clearer install logic.

Prefab profiles with utility-ready openings

Clean assembly with minimal field improvisation

FAQ

Builder questions that need direct answers.

Do you provide stamped structural packages?

Yes. ECOLIT can provide a structural framing package with stamped sheets where required by the project scope and jurisdiction.

Can my crew install it?

Yes. The default builder path is to use your crew with ECOLIT supervisor support, while turnkey erection is available when you want to hand over more of the field scope.

Do you support California permits / Title 24?

Yes. The permit package can include Title 24 input sheets together with assemblies, details, and structural logic aligned to California permit workflows.

What does the Inspection Pack include?

It includes a pre-inspection audit, inspection checklist, and same-day response path when inspector comments come in.

What happens if the inspector has comments?

ECOLIT responds the same day and helps the builder move through clarification without letting the project stall.

Is white-label available?

Yes. Builder-facing and homeowner-facing support materials can be adapted for a white-label presentation path.

Can you lock pricing for the full project?

Price lock or quote lock can be structured for 90 days to protect approved scope while the job moves through planning and permitting.

Are utility openings pre-made?

Yes. Utility-ready openings can be prefabricated, with routing guidance for HVAC, ventilation, and sprinkler paths where required.

Do you provide turnkey erection?

Yes. Builders can choose between supervisor-backed self-performed install and turnkey erection handled by ECOLIT.

What coatings are available?

G90 hot-dip galvanized steel to ASTM A653/A653M is standard. Alternative coatings such as 55% aluminum-zinc to ASTM A792/A792M or zinc-aluminum-magnesium-coated steel are available on request, subject to engineering and availability.

How does the delivery guarantee work?

If ECOLIT misses the committed delivery window under the agreed terms, the frame package is discounted 20%.
